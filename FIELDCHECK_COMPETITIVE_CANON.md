# FieldCheck Competitive Landscape — CANON (from May 2026 comparison + uncapped UTR model)
_Source: FieldCheck_Competitive_Comparison.pdf (May 2026) + SCALE_MODEL_V2 + STRATEGY_SYNTHESIS._
_This is the competitive spine for the GENERIC FieldCheck VC deck (not volleyball-specific)._

## THE POSITIONING (locked)
FieldCheck is NOT another recruiting profile, rankings DB, or video platform.
FieldCheck IS **the athlete intelligence layer** — identity, fit, development path, proof, personalized comparison.
The anti-ranking: not "where do you rank," but "who are you, how good can you be, what path fits you."
The MEANING layer on top of stats, video, rankings, and subjective coach feedback.

## THE WEDGE (what only FieldCheck owns)
- **Athlete identity / persona** — who the athlete IS (style, strengths, weaknesses), not a star rating.
- **Hero comps / player archetypes** — who you compare to, across sports.
- **Development roadmap** — what path fits, the next step.
- **Fit intelligence** — where you fit, before outreach.
- **The honest uncapped number** (UTR-style) — the proof layer under all of it.
Incumbents own exposure, workflow, stats, video, networks. NONE own identity+fit+development+proof.

## THE COMPETITIVE SET (the full landscape — generic, all sports)
| Platform | What it is | Strength | Weak where | FieldCheck edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NCSA** | Recruiting service/marketplace | Brand, coach access, family support | Service-heavy, not personalized to identity/development | AI-native, player-first, insight-driven |
| **FieldLevel** | Coach-athlete recruiting network | Coach-to-coach discovery | Profile/exposure oriented | The intelligence layer BEFORE outreach |
| **Hudl** | Video, highlights, team analysis | Massive video infra, trusted adoption | Footage, not recruiting-fit or persona | Sit on top of video → identity, comps, strategy |
| **SportsRecruits** | Recruiting profile/messaging | Profiles, coach DB, school search | Workflow/exposure, shallow player modeling | AI identity, hero path, fit logic |
| **Stack Athlete** | Broad recruiting platform | Education, visibility, connection | Older category feel | Premium, modern, personalized |
| **MaxPreps** | HS scores/rankings/stats | Broad public data | Not recruiting intelligence | Public data → private intelligence + action plans |
| **247 / Rivals / On3** | Recruiting media, star rankings | Huge recognition (FB/BB) | Elite/ranked only; useless for the long tail | Serve the serious long tail — clarity, development, fit |
| **BallerTV** | Event livestream/archive | Great capture layer | Raw video, not intelligence | Consume video/stat → the "so what": identity, proof, fit |
| **iStatVball / sport-stat tools** | Sport-specific stats | Purpose-built capture | Narrow, stats not identity | Stats as INPUT → persona, comps, roadmap |

## WHERE EACH WINS TODAY (and FieldCheck's path)
- Recruiting network → NCSA/FieldLevel/SportsRecruits. FC path: partner/integrate, or wedge sport/community.
- Video infra → Hudl. FC path: don't compete; ingest/complement.
- Public HS data → MaxPreps. FC path: convert public stats to private insight.
- **Athlete identity/persona → FieldCheck (unowned by incumbents). Make it the signature.**
- **Hero comps/pathing → FieldCheck (very differentiated if backed by real data).**
- **Player/parent emotional hook → FieldCheck ("who am I, how good can I be, what path fits me?").**
- **Long-term moat → OPEN. Own the player graph: stats + video + attributes + style + fit + outcomes.**

## THE MOAT TO BUILD (the one-liner)
A **player graph** that combines stats + video + physical attributes + style + development path + recruiting outcomes —
the calibrated, uncapped, honest intelligence layer nobody else is building.
