01 · The Problem

Sports evaluation is broken — and it fails the people who need it most.

Every athlete has a number in someone's head. Nobody will tell them the honest one, and no universal honest scale exists. The market runs on access, exposure, and politics — not truth.

Recruiting services
Sell hope to anxious parents
$1,200–3,000/yr for exposure and encouragement. The business model is the conflict of interest. Coaches distrust the ratings because they're paid for.
Rankings & mock drafts
Built for the top 1%
247, Rivals, On3 treat pedigree as potential. Star ratings for the recruited elite — and nothing for the 99% who play just as hard in ZIP codes nobody scouts.
Film & stats
Show what happened, never what you are
Hudl gave everyone footage. But footage isn't an answer — and someone has to upload you before you even exist. A library, not a verdict.
Tennis solved this. It's called UTR — one honest number every player has, amateur to pro, that the whole sport trusts.
Every other sport has nothing like it. No universal, honest, dynamic rating. That absence is the opportunity — and it's enormous: ~8 million U.S. high-school athletes alone, and not one of them can get a straight answer about where they stand.

02 · The Product

One honest number. Then the identity behind it.

FieldCheck's AI ingests the public record — stats, video, context — and returns a calibrated rating, then tells the athlete who they are, who they play like, and the path that fits. The film shows what happened. Our AI says how good you are, and how good you can be.

The number
Uncapped, UTR-style
One rating per athlete on an open-ended scale — no artificial ceiling. GOATs land where they naturally land, the way Novak sits at ~16 in UTR. Men, women, any sport, one continuous ruler. Graded across 8 facets vs the sport's greatest. Tough — every point earned.
The identity
Who you are, who you play like
Style, strengths, weaknesses — the persona no ranking gives you. Plus hero comps: the archetypes and greats you resemble, across sports. Not a star rating. A mirror.
The path
The two facets that move you up
A development roadmap and a fit read — where you stand, what moves your number, and which programs fit. The answer to "how good can I be, and what path fits me?"
Click any amateur and get a real read — before they ever sign up. We already see them.
The same engine grades a 12-year-old in a town nobody scouts and a four-time Olympian, on the same scale. That's the product: the honest number, free to run, plus the intelligence layer — identity, comps, path, fit — that no incumbent owns.
8 facets
Four you can see, four you can't
Reach, Power, Control, Court IQ — plus Compete, Mental, Mindset, Leadership. The invisible four are where careers are actually made, and where film is blind.
Free forever
The number costs nothing
Run any athlete, any sport, no signup. An honest measurement you have to pay for isn't honest. You pay for the tools built on top.
Agents
The intelligence does the labor
Six live agents — Scout, Editor, Strategist, Developer, Coverage, Discovery — do the work recruiting services charge humans for.
