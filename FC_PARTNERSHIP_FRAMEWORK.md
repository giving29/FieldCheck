# FieldCheck — Partnership Framework
**Kevin first. Reusable for everyone we pull in (Katlyn, Karan, and beyond.)**
_Core principle: pay for delivery, not for the relationship. Real roles, earned-in, milestone-weighted._

---

## THE PHILOSOPHY (applies to everyone)
1. **We own the core.** FieldCheck owns the engine, the data moat, the product, the company.
   Partners accelerate distribution, capital, and reach. The asset is ours; partners make it spread faster.
2. **Structure pays for delivery.** A real base that says "you're a partner," plus milestone accelerators
   that fire when they deliver the specific things only they can. Aligns to outcomes, not titles.
3. **Earned-in, not gifted.** Vesting + cliffs. Lock the structure as we ship together — even one sprint
   tells us volumes. Protects against the #1 founder mistake: over-granting on enthusiasm.
4. **Clear, non-overlapping roles.** Every partner has a lane only they own. No two people paid for the
   same value.

### The three tiers (what determines the number)
- **Advisor** (doors, name, occasional calls): 0.25%–1%
- **Key partner / operating advisor** (carries a relationship, actively helps raise, real ongoing work, not full-time): 1%–3%
- **Co-founder** (full-time-ish, owns a domain, in the trenches): 5%–15%+

Kevin talks like tier 2 → tier 3, but hasn't delivered yet. Structure for tier 2 now with a clear path to 3.

### KEVIN — recommended structure (STRATEGIC PARTNER — not a co-founder)
**Framing:** Sridhar is the sole founder and owns the core (engine, data, product, company).
Kevin is a **strategic partner** brought in to accelerate a defined lane (LOVB / GTM / capital).
Title can be "Founding Partner" or "Strategic Partner" — senior-sounding status, but on paper it is a
strategic-partner equity grant, NOT co-ownership. Paperwork says "strategic partner / advisor grant,"
never "co-founder." Zero ambiguity later.

**The principle: protect the base equity, give milestone bumps to a hard cap.**

- **Base: 2%** · vest **3 years** · **1-year cliff.** Meaningfully above advisor level = honors "central,"
  without co-founder dilution. This is the protected floor.
- **Milestone bumps (earned on delivery), capped at +3% total → hard cap of 5% all-in:**
  - Lands the LOVB partnership (signed) → **+1%**
  - Materially helps close the seed round → **+1%** (or scale to $ raised)
  - Opens club expansion (first 3+ clubs live) → **+1%**
- **HARD CAP: 5% all-in.** No path above 5% under this structure. If Kevin ever goes true
  full-time/co-founder-level, that's a separate, future renegotiation from a position of proof.

**Kevin helps DEFINE the milestones.** Bring him into naming what "meaningful" looks like — it gets his
buy-in, surfaces what he actually believes he can deliver, and makes the accelerators feel earned, not
imposed. Guardrails Sridhar keeps: milestones must be (a) specific and measurable, (b) genuinely
attributable to Kevin, (c) tied to real value (signed deals, dollars raised, clubs live — not activity
or effort), and (d) within the 5% hard cap no matter how many are defined.

**The angles / why this structure protects Sridhar while being generous to Kevin:**
- Base is small enough to protect the cap table, large enough to feel like a real partnership.
- Bumps are big and real — Kevin earns near-max only by delivering the things that actually build the company.
- The cap means enthusiasm can't run the number up; delivery is the only lever.
- Vesting + cliff means a short-lived partnership costs little; a long, productive one earns fully.
- Co-founder title avoided → control, ownership, and founding docs stay with Sridhar.
