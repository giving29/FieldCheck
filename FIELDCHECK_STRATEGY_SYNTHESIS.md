# FieldCheck — Strategy Synthesis (Problem · Market · Options · Our Solve)
_Synthesized from: FIELDCHECK_BIBLE, DATA_MOAT_DOCTRINE, thesis, CURATED_DB_FINDINGS, DECISIONS, SCALE_MODEL_V2._
_Purpose: the grounding doc for the VC deck. FieldCheck only._

## 1. THE PROBLEM
Every athlete has a number in someone's head — a coach's, a scout's, a recruiter's — but nobody
will tell them the honest one, and no honest one exists on a universal scale.
- **The market runs on information asymmetry.** Recruiting is a market; the athletes with the best
  access (private coaches, club networks, paid recruiting services, college counselors) win it.
  The kid without that access — rural, first-gen, D3, wrong ZIP code — is invisible, regardless of talent.
- **Everyone is incentivized to lie or flatter.** Coaches flatter to retain. Recruiters ghost.
  Rankings are politics and pay-to-play. Paid services sell hope to anxious parents.
- **The existing "answers" are broken:**
  - Rankings (247/Rivals/On3): pedigree-biased, top-heavy, treat potential = pedigree, ignore 99% of athletes.
  - Recruiting services (NCSA, $1,200–3,000/yr): sell exposure/hope to parents; coaches distrust the ratings; it's a lead-gen business, not a measurement.
  - Film (Hudl): shows *what happened*, never *what you are*. Someone has to upload you before you exist.
- **The core gap:** there is no universal, honest, dynamic rating that every athlete has —
  amateur to pro, any sport, men and women — that the whole ecosystem trusts. Tennis has UTR. Nobody else does.

## 2. THE MARKET
- Sports tech is a large, growing market built on *capturing* athletic data (footage, stats, tags).
  Nobody turned the capture into a trustworthy *answer*.
- **Users, four sides of one number:**
  - Players & families — "where do I actually stand, and what moves me up?" (anxious, underserved, will pay)
  - Coaches — "show me the whole board on one scale without watching 40 hours of film"
  - Scouts & franchises — "find the climber before the market prices them in"
  - Investors — "graded amateur performance data at scale is a dataset nobody has"
- **Incumbents & why they can't pivot:**
  - Hudl = film room. Owns footage, not judgment. Structurally a library.
  - NCSA/recruiting services = lead-gen to parents. Their business model IS the conflict of interest.
  - 247/Rivals/On3 = pedigree rankings for the top 1%. Can't and won't cover the amateur long tail.
  - UTR = proof the model works (universal rating, trusted, network effects) — but tennis-only.
- **The opening:** be the UTR of *every* sport — the universal honest rating layer everything else plugs into.

## 3. THE OPTIONS (what we could have built, and why we didn't)
- **A ranking** (be a better 247): rejected — rankings are relative, pedigree-biased, top-heavy, and adversarial to the mission of not writing anyone off.
- **A film tool** (compete with Hudl): rejected — capital-heavy, commoditized, and it's the wrong layer (footage ≠ answer).
- **A recruiting service** (a cleaner NCSA): rejected — the business model is the conflict of interest; charging athletes to be seen violates the firewall.
- **A single-sport rating** (UTR for volleyball): rejected as an endpoint — too small; the moat is being cross-sport and universal.
- **CHOSEN: the honest, uncapped, universal rating layer** — a refinery that reads the public record and returns one calibrated number per athlete, any sport, free to run, that gets more trusted the more it's used.

## 4. OUR SOLVE
**FieldCheck is the athletic intelligence layer — the UTR of every sport.**
- **The number:** one honest read per athlete, UNCAPPED UTR-style scale (no 10-ceiling; GOATs land
  where they naturally land; men/women/any sport on one continuous ruler), graded across 8 facets
  (4 visible + 4 invisible) vs the sport's GOAT, blind to jersey/money/name. Tough grading — every point earned.
- **The refinery (the how):** ingest four feedstock layers — L1 structured public data, L2 scraps,
  L3 existing video in the wild, L4 player uploads — and refine into calibrated reads.
- **The moat = Coverage × Calibration × Utility (all three at once):**
  - Coverage — "we already see you," before you sign up, including the amateurs nobody scouts.
  - Calibration — Loop B: "a FieldCheck 7.4 reaches D1 at X%," validated against real outcomes. Pay-to-play can't fake this.
  - Utility — six live agents (Scout, Editor, Strategist, Developer, Coverage, Discovery) do the labor
    recruiting services charge humans for.
- **The Clips Moat flywheel:** players volunteer graded amateur performance (uploads) because their
  incentive (exposure/status) equals ours (proprietary training data). The incentive is aligned — that's the engine.
- **The firewall = the brand:** free-tier-forever, no payola, six constraints we will never violate
  (never charge an athlete to see their own verdict, never sell placement, never take biased ads, etc.).
  In a market of conflicts of interest, being the one that refuses to lie IS the differentiation.
- **Why now:** AI makes the refinery possible solo/lean; incumbents are locked into their models;
  UTR proved the universal-rating category works and is investable.
