## THE ONE-LINE THESIS
FieldCheck is the independent talent-intelligence engine. LOVB is the wedge, the proof, and the first
platform customer. The differentiation-vs-MLV play is the door. The whole-league infrastructure is the house.

---

## THE FIVE HORIZONS
Each horizon is a level of the same company. We don't need permission for all of it up front — each
horizon earns the next. But we hold the whole picture so every sprint points somewhere.

### HORIZON 1 — DIFFERENTIATION (the wedge Kevin named first)
**What:** An independent number proving LOVB is the superior league vs MLV — position by position,
roster by roster, whole league.
**Why it wins:** MLV can't buy an outside number that says they're better. LOVB can just *be* better and
let the independent engine show it. Marketing is a claim; FieldCheck is proof.
**Who uses it:** the league (sponsor decks, press, chairman conversations), fans (the story of the gap).
**Revenue seed:** league-level licensing of the differentiation asset.

### HORIZON 2 — THE WHOLE-LEAGUE TALENT GRAPH
**What:** Every LOVB athlete graded, tracked over time, on one honest scale. Not a one-off study — a
living talent graph the league owns.
**Why it wins:** becomes the shared source of truth for who's good, who's rising, who fits where.
**Who uses it:** league office, coaches, GMs, recruiting.
**Revenue:** per-team / league SaaS; the graph is the product.

### HORIZON 3 — THE CLUBS (Kevin's ticket-sales insight)
**What:** Open it to every club. Each club gets its graded roster + fan-facing angle ("see how your
club stacks up," rivalry storylines, player-vs-player).
**Why it wins:** Kevin's exact point — this drives **ticket sales** and fan engagement. Unlimited angles:
rivalries, matchups, "the number says tonight's game is closer than the standings suggest."
**Who uses it:** clubs (marketing, ticketing), fans (engagement), broadcast (storylines).
**Revenue:** club subscriptions; fan-facing premium; sponsorship of the "FieldCheck rating" as a fixture.

### HORIZON 4 — THE PIPELINE (development + recruiting)
**What:** Extend the graph down into development — college, juniors, international. Who's coming up,
who LOVB should recruit, how players are progressing. The recruiting + development intelligence layer.
**Why it wins:** LOVB competes for talent; an independent development-tracking engine is a real edge.
**Who uses it:** LOVB recruiting, club development staff, agents.
**Revenue:** recruiting-intelligence SaaS; the pipeline data compounds the moat.

### HORIZON 5 — THE STANDARD (beyond LOVB)
**What:** FieldCheck becomes the independent talent number for volleyball globally — then the model
proves out and extends to other sports (the FieldCheck all-sports thesis).
**Why it wins:** once a league runs on it, it's infrastructure. Other leagues, sports, and media license it.
**Who uses it:** other leagues, media (broadcast overlays), betting/prediction (the EdgeCheck tie), the world.
**Revenue:** the platform. Multi-league, multi-sport, media + data licensing. This is the $1B version.
