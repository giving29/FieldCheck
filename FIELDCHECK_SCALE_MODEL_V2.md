# FieldCheck Scale Model V2 — THE UNCAPPED UTR MODEL
_Codified by Sridhar, July 2026. This SUPERSEDES the old "10 = unreachable, GOAT = 9.3 ceiling" model._
_Status: CANON. The old bounded 0–10 cap is RETIRED._

## THE CHANGE (one line)
No more 10.0 ceiling. FieldCheck goes UTR-style: an OPEN-ENDED scale with NO CAP.
The GOATs land where they naturally land — the way Novak sits at ~16 in UTR — and everyone
else falls out naturally beneath them. Men, women, any sport, one continuous uncapped scale.

## WHAT THIS MEANS
- **No artificial ceiling.** The number is not bounded at 10. It extends as greatness demands.
- **GOATs land naturally.** We do NOT pin Jordan at 9.3 against a 10-wall. The scale has headroom
  above even the current greatest; a true generational outlier can push the top higher.
- **UTR is the template.** UTR runs ~1–16.5 in practice, Novak/Nadal peaking ~16.2–16.4, and the
  top 100 in the world sit in a tight band near the top. FieldCheck adopts the same *shape*:
  continuous, opponent/level-adjusted, decimals matter, greatness concentrated at the top,
  but the ceiling is not a hard wall — it's wherever the best naturally land.
- **One scale for everyone.** Men and women, every sport, amateur to GOAT, all on the same
  continuous uncapped ruler. A 12-year-old and an Olympian are comparable on it.
- **Tough grading stays.** The stringency discipline is UNCHANGED and central: every point is
  enormous, decimals are earned, most athletes are low, 8+ is extremely rare air. The scale
  being uncapped does NOT mean inflation — it means the top is open, not that the middle rises.

## WHAT CARRIES OVER (unchanged from prior canon)
- Tough, honest, stringent grading — the number must be hard-earned.
- 8 facets (Reach, Power, Control, Court IQ, Compete, Mental, Mindset, Leadership) — 4 visible + 4 invisible.
- Graded vs the sport's GOAT anchor; blind to jersey/money/name.
- Free-tier-forever, no payola, the six constraints (the firewall).
- Refinery doctrine: L1–L4 feedstock → Coverage × Calibration × Utility.
- The Clips Moat: players volunteer graded amateur data because exposure (their incentive) = our data (our moat).
- Tier bands: ICON · ELITE+ · ELITE · STAR · PROSPECT · SCOUT (bands re-anchor to the uncapped scale).

## THE ALGORITHM WORK = LAB, PARKED
Getting the uncapped scale calibrated right (where do the GOATs land, how does the density
distribute, how do bands map to the open top) is HARD algorithm work. It is PARKED as LAB work,
separate from the site/positioning work. Do NOT try to re-grade everyone on the new scale as part
of the deck/site push — that's the lab track. The site + deck reflect the *model* and *story*;
the engine recalibration to the uncapped scale is its own dedicated lab session.

## IMPACT ON EXISTING NUMBERS (note for later)
- Current curated composites (Larson 9.3, Skinner 7.3, etc.) were on the OLD 0–10 model.
- When the uncapped scale is calibrated in lab, these get re-derived and will likely shift
  (the GOATs move up toward their natural landing spots; the tough middle stays tough).
- Until then: site/deck can present the *model* (uncapped, UTR-style) without re-pinning every number.

## WHY IT'S BETTER (for the VC story)
- No dead-end at 10 — every athlete always has room to climb (motivation + longitudinal value).
- Mirrors the most trusted rating in sports (UTR) — instantly legible to any sports VC.
- The scale never saturates at the top, so the GOAT tier stays meaningful forever.
- Uncapped + tough = "the honest, universal, open-ended rating every athlete has" — the UTR of every sport.
