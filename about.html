About FieldCheck

Why FieldCheck exists.

FieldCheck is the athlete intelligence layer — one honest, uncapped rating for every athlete, in every sport, plus the identity and path behind it. Built to give the kid nobody scouts the same straight answer as the number-one recruit in the country.

The straight answer nobody is paid to give.

Sports evaluation runs on access, exposure, and politics. The families with private coaches and paid services win; everyone else is invisible. FieldCheck exists to break that — to read the public record for every athlete and return a calibrated, honest number, free, no matter who you are or where you play.

It is one instrument across every sport — the same engine grades a twelve-year-old in a town nobody scouts and a four-time Olympian. Not a ranking. Not exposure for sale. A measurement.

The number

One number. No ceiling.

FieldCheck gives every athlete a single honest rating — graded across eight facets against the greatest to ever play the sport. There is no cap at ten. The GOATs land where they naturally land, the way Novak sits near sixteen in tennis’s UTR, and everyone else falls honestly beneath them.

One continuous scale — men, women, any sport, amateur to GOAT. Tough by design: every point is earned, and the number always has room to climb. The rating you can’t buy, inflate, or fake.

Who built this

Who built this.

Built by one operator, not a committee. Sridhar Nallani — twenty-five years building and running technology at scale, including CIO and CTO roles across Macy’s, Gap, Microsoft, and PROG Holdings. FieldCheck is built the way the best products are: close to the problem, fast, and without the layers that dull the truth.

The moat was never the code. It is the calibration, the data, the scoring philosophy, and the commitments below — the things that don’t copy.

Seven commitments

Seven commitments.

  1. We never charge an athlete to see their own verdict. The number is free, forever.
  2. We never take money to inflate a read. No pay-to-rank, no sponsored scores, ever.
  3. We publish our methodology. How the number is built is open to examination.
  4. Thin data is labeled thin. When the record is incomplete, we say so — we don’t dress it up.
  5. Amateurs matter as much as stars. The kid nobody scouts gets the same instrument as the No. 1 recruit.
  6. Corrections are logged, not hidden. When we’re wrong, we fix it in the open.
  7. We never sell athlete data. The graph exists to serve the athlete, not to be sold out from under them.
Independent

Independent. No conflicts.

No conflicts. No investors steering the read. No league, agency, or program can buy a better number. In a market built on conflicts of interest, refusing to lie is the whole point — and the reason the number is worth trusting.
